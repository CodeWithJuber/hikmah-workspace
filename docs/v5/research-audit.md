The file expressly excludes claims that all languages descend from Quranic Arabic; roots encode every meaning; roots prescribe algorithms; numerical letter assignments prove intelligence/compression; the research already achieves near-AGI; or external transformer internals become non-transformer through a symbolic companion. Preserve these limits.

The [Quranic Arabic Corpus morphology documentation](https://corpus.quran.com/documentation/morphologicalfeatures.jsp) independently supports the distinction between roots, lemmas, morphology and contextual meaning, including parts of speech outside root-plus-template representation. Frequency in one corpus does not establish semantic importance, a causal law, historical origins or universal completeness. Any such research hypothesis needs defined units, normalization, controls, held-out data and counterexamples.
